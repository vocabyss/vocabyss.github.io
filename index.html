Learn words by fighting them.

Vocabyss is a roguelike dungeon crawler for vocabulary. Every monster is a word: answer to strike, miss and it strikes back. Spaced repetition decides which words come back, and when.

  • Five languages to learn — English, Spanish, German, French and Ukrainian — from six interface languages.
  • Short runs of 7–9 minutes, a daily goal, a hero who levels up, relics and outfits.
  • The whole course is free. Pro removes ads.

Vocabyss is in closed beta on Android. Questions, bug reports or ideas: vocabyss.team@gmail.com.
